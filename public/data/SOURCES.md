# 数据来源与授权 / Data sources and licences

KetikLab 的源代码采用 MIT 许可证（见仓库根目录 `LICENSE`）。本目录下的数据文件**不**因此改为 MIT，
各自沿用下列来源的授权。
KetikLab's source code is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE` at the repository root). The data files in this
directory are **not** relicensed by that; each keeps the licence of its source, as listed below.

KetikLab 的词库，除下方单独标注来源的文件外，均来自可自由再分发的开放数据，或由项目自行编写。
Except where a file below states otherwise, KetikLab's word libraries come from openly
redistributable data or are authored in-house.

## 英语词库 / English libraries

`en-core.json` · `en-plus.json` · `en-upper.json` · `en-academic.json`

由 `scripts/build-en-dicts.py` 生成，可完整复现。
Generated by `scripts/build-en-dicts.py`; fully reproducible.

| 用途 | 来源 | 授权 |
|---|---|---|
| 英文释义 English definitions | [Open English WordNet 2024](https://en-word.net/) | CC BY 4.0 |
| 中文释义 Chinese glosses | [Chinese Open Wordnet (omw-cmn 1.4)](https://github.com/omwn) | WordNet License |
| 印尼语释义 Indonesian glosses | [Wordnet Bahasa (omw-id 1.4)](https://github.com/omwn) | MIT |
| 词频分档 Frequency banding | [wordfreq 3.x](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) | Apache-2.0 |
| 美式音标 US pronunciation | [CMUdict](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict) | BSD-2-Clause |

以上四个词表按词频 Zipf 值分档，不使用任何考试机构的官方词表。
Those four libraries select by objective Zipf frequency bands. No examination board's
official word list is used in them, and none uses an examination trademark as its name.

`en-business.json`

词条、中文释义与印尼语释义**全部由本项目自行编写**（商务语域），不含任何第三方数据。
Headwords and both the Chinese and Indonesian glosses are **entirely authored in-house**
for business register. This file contains no third-party data.

`en-toefl.json`

由 `scripts/build-toefl-dict.py` 从第三方托福备考材料《2026 BEAT 托福必考2000词》PDF 提取。
**该来源是商业备考课程的配套材料，未附任何再分发授权**，与本文件其余条目不同，不属于开放数据；
其名称亦使用了考试商标。
Extracted by `scripts/build-toefl-dict.py` from a third-party TOEFL preparation PDF, the
companion material of a commercial course. **That source carries no redistribution licence**
and, unlike everything else listed here, is not open data; its name also uses an
examination trademark.

| 用途 | 来源 | 授权 |
|---|---|---|
| 词头与中文释义 Headwords and Chinese glosses | 上述第三方 PDF / the third-party PDF | 未获授权 / none established |
| 美式音标 US pronunciation | 本站 en-* 词库 / this site's en-* libraries ([CMUdict](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict)) | BSD-2-Clause |
| 印尼语释义 Indonesian glosses | 本站 en-* 词库 / this site's en-* libraries ([Wordnet Bahasa](https://github.com/omwn)) | MIT |

提取时已排除该来源的助记内容（【词根】【拓展】【词源】【辨析】【形近】）及其音标转写；
音标与印尼语释义改由本站既有的开放词库补齐，2100 词中分别覆盖 1463 词与 1294 词。
The source's mnemonics and its own phonetic transcriptions are excluded. Pronunciations and
Indonesian glosses come instead from this site's existing open libraries, covering 1463 and
1294 of the 2100 entries.

## 中文与印尼语词库 / Chinese and Indonesian libraries

`zh-core.json` · `zh-plus.json` · `zh-upper.json`

由 `scripts/build-zh-dicts.py` 生成，可完整复现：按 wordfreq 的中文词频选词，释义经 ILI 在三个开放词网之间对齐。
Generated by `scripts/build-zh-dicts.py`; fully reproducible. Headwords are selected by
Chinese corpus frequency and the glosses are aligned across three open wordnets through ILI.

| 用途 | 来源 | 授权 |
|---|---|---|
| 中文词头 Chinese headwords | [Chinese Open Wordnet (omw-cmn 1.4)](https://github.com/omwn) | WordNet License |
| 英文释义与定义 English glosses and definitions | [Open English WordNet 2024](https://en-word.net/) | CC BY 4.0 |
| 印尼语释义 Indonesian glosses | [Wordnet Bahasa (omw-id 1.4)](https://github.com/omwn) | MIT |
| 词频选词 Frequency selection | [wordfreq 3.x](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) | Apache-2.0 |
| 拼音 Pinyin | [pypinyin](https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin) | MIT |

`indonesian.json` · `zh-pinyin.json` — 由本项目自行编写与生成。
Authored and generated in-house.

`zh-pinyin.json` 的拼音由 [pypinyin](https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin)（MIT）生成，
难度分级由 wordfreq（Apache-2.0）的中文词频得出。

`words.json`（主题词汇 / Kosakata Tematik / Words by Topic）

前约 2,200 条由本项目自行编写；其后的条目由 `scripts/build-trio-queue.py` 每日生成，并经人工校订。
The first ~2,200 entries are authored in-house; later entries are generated daily by
`scripts/build-trio-queue.py` and reviewed by hand.

| 用途 | 来源 | 授权 |
|---|---|---|
| 生成条目的词头与选词 Generated headwords and selection | [Open English WordNet 2024](https://en-word.net/) · [wordfreq 3.x](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) | CC BY 4.0 · Apache-2.0 |
| 生成条目的中文释义 Chinese glosses of generated entries | [Chinese Open Wordnet (omw-cmn 1.4)](https://github.com/omwn) | WordNet License |
| 生成条目的印尼语释义 Indonesian glosses of generated entries | [Wordnet Bahasa (omw-id 1.4)](https://github.com/omwn) | MIT |
| 美式音标 US pronunciation | [CMUdict](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict) | BSD-2-Clause |
| 拼音 Pinyin | [pypinyin](https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin) | MIT |

## 阅读材料 / Reading passages

`readings.json` — 仅收录公共领域（public domain）文本，每篇的 `note` 字段标注作者、年代、来源与授权状态。
Public-domain texts only; each entry's `note` field records author, era, source and licence status.
